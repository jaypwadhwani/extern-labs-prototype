Step 3 of 6
Mini Task — User Research Synthesis
The data shows 68% of users drop off at "Invite Your Team."
You pulled the funnel analytics. The drop-off is concentrated at one step. Now you read the latest user feedback from churned sign-ups. Read all five, then identify the most important insight.
TK
Taylor K.  ·  Signed up 12 days ago
"The onboarding tutorial moved too fast. I couldn't keep up and eventually just gave up."
MJ
Morgan J.  ·  Signed up 8 days ago

AR
Alex R.  ·  Signed up 5 days ago
"I signed up thinking I could get started right away, but it turns out my company already has a Meridian workspace and I need my admin to add me. I had no way of knowing that when I signed up."
SL
Sam L.  ·  Signed up 18 days ago
"Everything was fine but the confirmation email took forever to arrive. I almost gave up."
PE
Parker E.  ·  Signed up 3 days ago
"The interface looked great but the 'Invite Your Team' step felt way too early. I just wanted to explore first."
Your analysis
Which insight best explains the 68% drop-off at "Invite Your Team"?
You're looking for the structural reason, not the surface complaint.
A
The tutorial moves too fast
Users lose confidence early and don't make it to team setup.
B
The interface is too overwhelming
Too many options on first login causes users to drop before completing setup.
C
Users don't realize they need admin access
Most new sign-ups can't complete workspace setup — they need an existing admin to invite them first.
D
Team setup is too early in the flow
The "Invite Your Team" step should come later, after users have explored the product.
What You Know

"Invite Your Team" step

💡 PM Tip
The best insight isn't the loudest complaint — it's the one that explains why a specific step in the funnel breaks. Ask: which of these causes people to stop at exactly this point?

What do you build?
Step 4 of 6
Decision 2 of 4 — Prioritization
Hypothesis confirmed. Engineering has 1 sprint. What do you ship?
The data and user feedback align: most new sign-ups aren't workspace admins. They hit "Invite Your Team," realize they can't proceed, and leave. You have one sprint. What's your recommendation?
A
Rebuild the onboarding flow end-to-end
Design a completely separate onboarding path for non-admin users. Estimated: 6 weeks.
B
Add a callout at signup explaining admin requirements
Surface a clear message early: "Your company may already have a workspace. Check with your IT admin." Estimated: 3 days.
C
Survey 100 more users to validate first
Run a targeted survey before touching the product to confirm this is the root cause. Estimated: 1 week.
D
Build an email drip for non-admin users
Auto-detect when a sign-up is likely non-admin and send a guide email while they wait for access. Estimated: 2 weeks.

Decision 3 of 4 — Sequencing

Your fix shipped 10 days ago. Activation improved 15%. The sprint cost 3 days of engineering time. Priya is asking what you want to tackle next. You have 1 more sprint before stakeholder review.
A
Commission a full visual redesign of onboarding

D
Investigate the next biggest drop-off in the funnel
Pull the updated funnel data and find the next step with the highest drop-off rate.
